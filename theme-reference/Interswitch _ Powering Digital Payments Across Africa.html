Our Journey

Two Decades Of Growth & Innovation

From our first breakthrough in 2005 to powering millions today, our journey is just beginning.

Our Story

2002-2006

Foundation Years

Interswitch was founded in 2002, pioneering Nigeria’s electronic payments ecosystem. We rolled out Paydirect, Webpay, and Autopay, powering the country’s most successful early digital products.

Foundation Years

2006-2010

Building Infrastructure

We spearheaded Nigeria’s migration to EMV standards and rolled out Quickteller. Verve, Africa’s first EMV domestic payment card scheme was also launched..

Building Infrastructure

2010-2014

Regional Expansion

Interswitch initiates partnerships with the Central Bank and Bankers Committees of Gambia and Sierra Leone on national switches. GamSwitch goes live, marking the first operational national switch in Gambia.

Regional Expansion

2014-2018

Diversification & Global Reach

Interswitch acquires VANSO, a mobile and security service provider. We relaunched the brand with a whole new identity. Verve Global cards commence international transactions on the Discover Network.

Diversification & Global Reach

2018-2022

Ecosystem Growth

Launched Africa’s first interoperable QR platform, strengthened Verve and Quickteller ecosytems, expanded agent networks, and enhanced mobility, healthcare and energy solutions, becoming a backbone for financial inclusion.

Ecosystem Growth

2022-2025

Innovation & Leadership

Advanced Africa’s digital economy with Smartfuel, Quickteller Homes, healthcare platforms and the re-launch of the API Marketplace, enabling developers and driving next-generation innovation.
